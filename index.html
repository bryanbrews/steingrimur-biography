Steingrímur Hermannsson — A Biography

The life of Iceland's Prime Minister, translated into English by his grandson. Read it — or hear it, including in Steingrímur's own voice.

  1. Tap an Open in Apple Books button below.

  3. Choose Open in Books (or tap Share ⎋ → Books).
