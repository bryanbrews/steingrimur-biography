Steingrímur Hermannsson — A Biography

Steingrímur Hermannsson (1928–2010) served twice as Prime Minister of Iceland (1983–87 and 1988–91). His biography, written in Icelandic by Dagur B. Eggertsson, is available here in English for the first time — an edition created by his grandson Bryan Hermannsson. Read it, or listen to it in three voices — including Steingrímur's own.
